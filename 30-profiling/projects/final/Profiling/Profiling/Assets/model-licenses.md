**game-scene.usda** is part of a full Unreal Engine scene that is public domain from http://www.polyhaven.com.
Caroline Begbie deleted most of the scene in Unreal Engine, then exported to USD format.

**fire-pit.usdz** is also public domain from http://www.polyhaven.com, as is the HDRI used for the sky.

The rocks were made by Caroline Begbie in Blender, and placed into the public domain.

**phoenix_bird.usdz**: “Phoenix Bird” 3D model by NORBERTO-3D, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
Model available at Sketchfab: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/phoenix-bird-844ba0cf144a413ea92c779f18912042

The textures were modified by Caroline Begbie to enable easier rendering.
